Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) describes how Dragify, Inc. (“Dragify,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information in connection with the Dragify mobile application (the “App”) and any related websites or services (collectively, the “Services”).

WELCOME TO DRAGIFY’S PRIVACY POLICY

Dragify is a creative photo transformation service that allows users to generate stylized, artistic portrait images using AI-based processing.

If you do not read this entire Policy, please understand these key points:

Dragify processes only the images you explicitly select for transformation.

We do not use your images for advertising, training unrelated models, or selling to third parties.

We use third-party cloud infrastructure to perform image processing.

Images are stored only temporarily for processing and performance purposes and are automatically deleted after a short retention window.

You remain in control of what you upload, delete, and share.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
1.1 Images You Provide

When you use the App to generate or modify an image, we temporarily process:

Photos or images you upload or capture through the App.

Generated images resulting from the transformation.

These images are processed only to provide the requested transformation. They are not used for advertising, sold, or shared for unrelated purposes.

Images are:

Transmitted securely to our processing servers

Stored only temporarily for processing and short-term caching

Automatically deleted within a limited time window (generally within 24–48 hours)

1.2 Usage and Technical Information

We may collect limited non-image information including:

App usage events (feature usage, session timing, error states)

Device and system data (OS version, device type, language, IP-based region)

Subscription and entitlement status (we do not collect billing or payment card data)

This data is associated with an internal, anonymous App Instance ID rather than your name or identity.

1.3 Communications

If you contact us (e.g., support email), we may collect:

Your email address

The contents of your message

Any information you voluntarily provide

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION
2.1 Images

We use images only to:

Perform the requested transformation

Return the generated result to you

Enable limited re-edits within the short retention window

We do not use your images to train unrelated AI models, for marketing, or for resale.

2.2 Other Information

We use non-image data to:

Operate and improve the App

Provide support and respond to requests

Maintain security and prevent abuse

Analyze aggregate usage patterns

Manage subscriptions and entitlements

Comply with legal obligations

3. HOW WE SHARE INFORMATION

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information with:

Authorities, when legally required
